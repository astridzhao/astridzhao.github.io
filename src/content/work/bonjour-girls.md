## Technology in service of community

I build and maintain community-facing software for Bonjour Girls, a women's community in New York. The work spans member records, event and volunteer operations, registration synchronization, and analytics for organizers.

The engineering focus is practical: helping a small team coordinate meaningful in-person experiences through reliable, usable tools. I have worked across backend APIs, databases, front-end flows, QA, and cloud deployment.

I care about making technology feel genuinely supportive to the people using it—not just shipping another dashboard.
