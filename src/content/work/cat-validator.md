## Making specifications executable

I pioneered a specification-first approach to building regulatory validation tools. Instead of translating requirements into implementation only through manual handoffs, the workflow connects specification interpretation, rules generation, and traceability checks.

The CAT Validator work covered approximately **8,900 rules** in a reporting environment processing around **8 million daily trades**. Our internal development process reached **100% mapped specification coverage** and reduced the development cycle by approximately **75%**.

## Beyond one project

I extended this thinking into AI-assisted specification-to-Jira workflows using MCP and Copilot-based tooling, and shared the approach at a technology expo.

This project reinforced my view that AI-assisted development works best when its output can be checked against explicit requirements—not just generated quickly.
